Welcome to Soilitary. Our mission is to make the gardening process simple by providing a straightforward, step-by-step guides that can help anyone learn how to grow their own food from cheap seeds while keeping cash in their pockets.

If you are looking to lower your weekly grocery bills, growing high-yield vegetables at home is one of the most effective ways to save money. A single packet of seeds costs less than a grocery store bundle of produce and can yield pounds of fresh, organic vegetables all season long. Our site includes breakdown lists of essential supplies, depth charts, watering schedules, and practical tips to maximize your harvest and your savings

The Power of a Single Seed Packet: See how much food security a tiny investment creates. If you look below at the actual yield potential of regular backyard crops when grown from scratch:

Tomato Seeds: A single ₱35.00 ($0.60) seed packet can yield over 15 to 20 kilograms of fresh tomatoes across the harvest season.

Basil Seeds: A single ₱35.00 ($0.60) seed packet produces continuous weekly herb harvests for months, regrowing every time you trim it.

Salad Green Seeds: A single ₱35.00 ($0.60) seed packet can provide up to 50 individual heads of leafy greens using a cut-and-come-again method.
