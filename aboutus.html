We chose the name “Soilitary” because right now, a lot of us feel like we are fighting a lonely, stressful battle just to keep up with the cost of living. In most countries, the price of everything has inflated. Every time you go to the market or the grocery store, you watch your hard-earned money buy less and less basic food. It’s incredibly exhausting.

With the help of this website, we can assure you that you will be able to save more money. You don’t need a huge yard or a lot of money to start. Even if you just use a few simple pots on a windowsill, you can grow your own food, half your grocery bill, and take control of your budget right now.

That is why we started building this site, we wanted to show that you don't need a huge community farm, a ton of space, or a lot of cash to start pushing back against inflation. You can start small, on your own, right where you are. By growing your own vegetables and herbs in simple pots, containers, or any patch of dirt you have, you can protect your wallet and take control of your daily budget. Soilitary is just us sharing straightforward, no-jargon tips to help everyday people save money by getting their hands in the dirt.
